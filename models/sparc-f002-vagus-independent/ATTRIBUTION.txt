Modifications: selected three existing source-rendered surfaces, converted JSON arrays to GLB, and applied the verified micrometer-to-meter scale (1e-6). Original positions, normals and face order were retained after float32 representation. Original display colors were retained with a Phong-to-PBR material approximation. Display labels simplify source surface names; exact sourceName values remain available.
The source-author fitted epineurium surfaces are approximate representations of an ex-vivo specimen. One anterior gastric branch origin is included; distal gastric distribution and in-body organ alignment are not supplied. No new geometry, anatomical registration, complete innervation mapping or endorsement is claimed.
