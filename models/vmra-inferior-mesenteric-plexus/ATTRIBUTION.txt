New illustrative nerve surfaces follow external corridors derived from original inferior mesenteric, left-colic and sigmoid arterial surfaces. Neural departures toward the composed compound bowel wall, thread positions, thickness and junctions are authored. This is not measured nerve segmentation. Original anatomical files remain unchanged.
Representative regional sympathetic contribution toward the descending and sigmoid regions of one intact compound bowel surface. Exact nerve fibers, caliber, complete innervation and a sharp internal territorial boundary are not measured or claimed. Upstream neural connections, pelvic parasympathetic pathways and intramural branches are not represented.
