FINAL REVIEW AND PACKAGE SCOPE
This package contains 23 source-derived right-intestinal, vascular, lymph-node and peritoneal surfaces. Each retained source mesh is marked CC-BY-SA-4.0. Above collection-level notices are preserved from upstream; this package contains no inner-ear, kidney, cranial-nerve or white-matter meshes. The original-conversion statements above describe the upstream assets; this derivative applies the separately disclosed spatial field.
Accepted for the bounded educational composition following independent exact exported-geometry review. Not measured anatomy, dimensional atlas accuracy, or clinical registration.
Educational source-derived body-model accommodation; not measured individual anatomy or clinical registration.
Native anterior-wall residual: ascending colon max sampled depth 0.986 mm over 1.118 cm2; greater omentum max sampled depth 1.937 mm over 1.542 cm2. These are retained model interpenetrations, not physiological contacts.
Existing source jejunal and vascular self-contact defects remain. Newly counted face-pair identities are confined to existing contact patches; no isolated new fold was identified.
Local shape and caliber change under the field: appendix volume ratio approximately 0.495 and appendicular artery approximately 0.511. Original dimensional or clinical caliber accuracy is not claimed.
Ascending colon is a double-wall source surface; appendix and jejunal ray chords do not establish anatomical wall thickness.
No new terminal branches, complete ileal/caecal compartment, or exact segment boundary is inferred from source names. Original superior anorectal artery remains fixed outside this field.
