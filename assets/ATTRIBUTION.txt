open3d-muscle-tile.jpg is an original JIN Anatomy Studio texture, synthesized by scripts/make_muscle_textures.ps1 from periodic noise (muscle fibres, fascicle seams, tendon streaks). License: CC BY-SA 4.0, like the modified model data it accompanies.

It replaces the UBC-derived Open3D/AnatomyTOOL "Muscle tile plain" image (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), which is not distributed because this app is sold. Only a heavily blurred muscle-versus-tendon layout mask and two mean colours were taken from that image so the new texture fits the same UV layout; no fibre detail was copied. The same replacement was made for every UBC-derived muscle/tendon image embedded in the Open3D GLBs (scripts/replace_ubc_muscle_textures.mjs).

The texture is projected on selected Z-Anatomy left-sided muscle surfaces for visual color and texture parity; Z-Anatomy source geometry and authored tendon-face masks remain unchanged. The display projection is a visualization technique, not evidence of individual fiber directions or microscopic anatomy.

In the connected-body presentation, individually reviewed muscle pairs use a reflected display copy: latissimus dorsi from the Open3D right; external oblique, rectus abdominis, rectus femoris, the three vasti, seven paired deep chest/upper abdominal muscles, and twelve paired back/limb muscles from the Z-Anatomy left. The seven chest/upper abdominal pairs are pectoralis minor, serratus anterior, external/internal/innermost intercostals, transversus thoracis, and internal abdominal oblique. The twelve back/limb pairs are splenius capitis, brachioradialis, serratus posterior superior, gluteus maximus/medius/minimus, soleus, fibularis longus, tibialis anterior, short head of biceps brachii, extensor carpi radialis longus, and levator scapulae. A further 16 named pairs across the back, upper limb, hip and lower limb use the better resolved Z-Anatomy left surface. The right pectoralis major, deltoid and trapezius overviews each combine and reflect three separately named Z-Anatomy left parts. Ten paired major bones also use a reflected display copy of the better resolved side: left Z-Anatomy scapula to the right, and right Open3D humerus, radius, ulna, clavicle, hip bone, femur, tibia, fibula and patella to the left. Thirteen additional foot-bone pairs use the better resolved Open3D right surface. Original source geometry remains in its model package. Reflected sides are display derivatives, not independently sourced anatomy; other structures retain their original source surfaces.

The connected-body overview displays Open3D right limb nerves and Z-Anatomy left limb nerves with one gold material for legibility and bilateral color consistency. Five individually reviewed left nerve surfaces (median, radial, ulnar, obturator and sciatic) use reflected display copies of their named Open3D right peers; the original left source geometry and materials remain in the source packages. The differing density of other branches between the two sources is not anatomically reconstructed.

For the optional connective and fascial layers, 86 right-only Open3D tendon, fascia and bursa support surfaces now have selectable left display reflections. Each uses the unchanged right source triangles and material with a reflected transform. These left surfaces are presentation derivatives, not independent atlas anatomy; the three specialized right inguinal fascia surfaces remain right-only.
